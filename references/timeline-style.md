# 时间轴与画面约束

Use named, logically grouped VSE strips. A project might separate background/recording, presenter PIP, dim layers, callouts, captions, narration, BGM, and effects onto consistent channels. Inspect the existing channel layout before adding strips; channel numbers from one project are not a universal standard.

## Screen recording and PIP

- Favor a large, legible recording. Place the presenter inside a safe part of the screen image if that is what the user wants; avoid taking a full side column merely for a small portrait.
- Treat circular crop, border, shadow, and presenter image as one timed unit. Every part appears and disappears together. Avoid a lingering circle after the presenter leaves.
- Keep the presenter's face large enough to read, but move it if it covers the task's input field, button, prompt, or result.
- For full-screen presenter segments, behind-person words require an actual foreground matte of the person. Split words or phrases to the spoken rhythm; review edge quality, especially glasses, hair, and hands.

## Captions, steps, and callouts

- Keep caption placement and size consistent within a section. Use a single active caption thought at a time. A shadow or thin outline can improve contrast without a heavy bar; follow the user's specific style choice.
- Check placement against platform UI, the recorder's cursor, Codex/input panels, and PIP. Mobile legibility matters even for a horizontal export.
- Step cards should describe the actual current step, and should return only where narration and screen action warrant them.
- For an important prompt or reply, enlarge the relevant text region or show a concise card; move smoothly into the close-up and return to the full interface. Do not indiscriminately enlarge the whole desktop.
- A material handoff can group genuine logo, covers, portrait, and QR into one card, then animate the card toward the context input. Use a subdued screen behind it when readability benefits.

## Audio

- Duck BGM under speech and preserve its original playback rate unless expressly requested otherwise. Loop or crossfade at musical boundaries instead of accelerating it to fit footage.
- Use whoosh on substantive transitions and a quiet click on a meaningful reveal or selection; avoid adding effects to every cut.
- Listen at dialogue cuts and at every transition between retimed and normal footage. Check that the spoken syllable, caption, PIP frame, and screen action belong to the same moment.
